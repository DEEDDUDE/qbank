## PH-Q-012
status: conflict
form: mcq
type: single
answer: b
claimed: d
basis: Ch. 9 — Levels of Prevention {#ch09-4}
evidence: Secondary prevention's own definition, word for word: "action which halts the progress of a disease at its incipient stage and prevents complications." Its objectives list repeats this exactly: "prevent complications and sequelae." Type 2 diabetes is an already-diagnosed disease here, and the action described is stopping its complications before they occur — precisely secondary prevention's job, not primary prevention, which by definition acts before disease onset.
note: Moodle's own status line read "not yet answered" for this question while a radio (Primary) was visibly selected — Job A recorded that selection as a real claim rather than discarding it. The circulating attempt marks Primary — a genuine error either way; primary prevention cannot apply to a disease that already exists. The source supports Secondary.

Preventing the complications of type 2 diabetes
a) Tertiary
b) Secondary
c) Primordial
d) Primary
