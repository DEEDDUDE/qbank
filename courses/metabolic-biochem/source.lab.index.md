# metabolic-biochem lab — index

Lab chapters only — see [source.index.md](source.index.md) for lecture chapters. Anchors
here are `{#labNN}`, never `{#chNN}`, so a citation can never mix the two tracks. Load
this file whole; load only the one lab chapter a question routes to.

## {#lab01} Lab 1 — Safety Rules and Basic Measurements
covers: PPE (lab coats, footwear, gloves, eyewear), chemical safety rules, general
laboratory safety poster rules, basic lab equipment (centrifuge types, analytical
balance, spectrophotometer, pH meter, pipette use and technique), phlebotomy procedure
and vein sites
terms: PPE, fume hood, centrifuge, ultracentrifuge, preparative vs analytical
ultracentrifuge, analytical balance, pH meter, automatic micropipette, pipette plunger
positions, phlebotomy, median cubital vein, cephalic vein, basilic vein, tourniquet

transmittance, absorbance, optical density, Beer's law, Lambert's law, extinction
coefficient, A max

## {#lab03} Lab 3 — Measurement of Plasma Enzymes: Alkaline and Acid Phosphatase

## {#lab04} Lab 4 — Liver Function Tests

## {#lab11} Lab 11 — Selected Clinical Cases

terms: Wilson's disease, Leipzig score, Kayser-Fleischer rings, ceruloplasmin, PIEZO1,
