### PHARM-P-001
status: verified
answer: a
basis: Ch. 1 — Introduction to Pharmacology {#ch01-1}
evidence: Pharmacokinetics is the study of absorption, distribution, metabolism,
  and excretion — "what the body does to a drug," i.e. how a drug reaches its
  target and how blood levels are affected by ADME factors.

### PHARM-P-002
status: verified
answer: d
basis: Ch. 1 — Introduction to Pharmacology {#ch01-4}
evidence: IV gives "precise, accurate, almost immediate onset" (predictable
  levels — a), and first-pass metabolism is specific to drugs taken by mouth
  (gut → portal vein → liver); IV bypasses this route entirely, so none of an
  IV dose undergoes first-pass metabolism (b).
