### MICRO-LAB-005
status: verified
answer: K-B test determines the zone of inhibition; the relationship between K-B and
  MIC is inverse — more susceptible means lower MIC and larger zone, more resistant
  means higher MIC and smaller zone.
basis: Lab 8 — Antibiotic Sensitivity Testing {#lab08-4}
evidence: "The zone size... is directly related to the log of that antibiotic's MIC
  for the tested organism — the smaller the zone, the higher the MIC, and the less
  susceptible the organism."
