### MICRO-Q-004

### MICRO-Q-034
status: verified
answer: b
basis: Ch. 12 — Viral Replication {#ch12}
evidence: HIV (a positive-polarity ssRNA retrovirus) is reverse-transcribed into a
  double-stranded DNA provirus; a host RNA polymerase then transcribes viral mRNA and
  progeny genome RNA from that DNA — by the basic rule of transcription, the DNA strand
  used as template (the negative-sense strand) yields the positive-sense RNA copies.
